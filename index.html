Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025

Welcome to our Privacy Policy. This policy applies to the following applications:

  • CardsKidsGame – An educational game for kids to create and match cards.
  • Strike Soccer Coach – A tactical board app for soccer coaches to illustrate strategies.

1. Information We Collect

Both apps prioritize privacy and do not collect any personal data. Below is a breakdown of data handling for each app:

CardsKidsGame

  • No personal data collection.
  • Custom cards and images are stored locally on the user’s device.
  • No internet connectivity required.
  • No third-party tracking or analytics.

Strike Soccer Coach

  • No personal data collection.
  • Saved tactics and images remain on the user’s device.
  • May use Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics (no personal data collected).

2. Children's Privacy

CardsKidsGame is designed for children and follows COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines:

  • No online data collection.
  • No advertisements or in-app purchases.

3. How We Use the Information

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no information to use or share.

In Strike Soccer Coach, anonymous analytics (such as app usage) may be used to improve performance.

4. Data Security

All user-generated content (cards, tactics, images) is stored locally on your device. If the app is uninstalled, all stored data will be lost.

5. Third-Party Services

  • CardsKidsGame: No third-party services are used.
  • Strike Soccer Coach: Google Analytics may be used to collect anonymous app usage data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here.
